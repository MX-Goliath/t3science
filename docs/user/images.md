# Images

Open **Images** in the sidebar or command palette to browse images generated by
your connected Codex accounts. The gallery includes the accounts' configured
home directories and groups remote images by environment. **Open directory**
opens the image folder on the environment that owns it.

Select an image to view it, zoom and drag it, download it, or open its original conversation
at the image's message. Older Codex sessions open in a conversation viewer when
they are not part of T3's saved history. If the original history was deleted,
the image remains available.

Use the standard composer below the gallery to describe a new image. When an
image is open, describe your changes there instead. Sending includes the viewed
image with your prompt in a new chat without a project, then opens that chat and keeps the original
conversation unchanged. The gallery's labels follow your browser's Russian or
English language preference.

On mobile, open the **Images** tab and choose a connected environment. Pinch or
double-tap a photo to zoom, then drag to move around it. **Save image** lets you
choose a folder on your device. The input starts collapsed and expands when you
tap it; sending changes opens a new chat without a project, just as on desktop.
